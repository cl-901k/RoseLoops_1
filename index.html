Common Questions & Care Guide

Everything you need to know about caring for handmade garments and ordering custom crochet designs.

To preserve the delicate stitches and elasticity, always hand wash cold with a mild wool detergent. Do not rub or wring. Lay flat on a clean dry towel away from direct sunlight. Never machine wash or hang dry, as this will stretch out the design.

Since each piece is hand-stitched by a single artisan, smaller accessories (like beanies, headbands, coasters) take 2-4 days, bags take 4-7 days, and full wearables (like cardigans and intricate tops) take 7-14 days. We will provide a precise delivery date when your order is placed.
